## Definition

Macket is a tool that helps computer shops and buyers source used MacBooks. It gathers listings from public Facebook groups focused on MacBooks and uses AI to identify posts likely to come from individual sellers. Buyers can filter by model and open the original post to contact the seller.

## The problem Macket solves

Finding a suitable MacBook often means checking several Facebook groups. The model, specifications, price, and condition appear in different parts of each post. The same listing can show up in multiple groups, and posts from individual sellers can be hard to distinguish from posts by stores and resellers. A well-priced device may sell while a buyer is sorting through those posts.

Macket gathers listings in one place, checks for duplicates, and classifies sellers. The `Skip Author` list lets later collection runs exclude posts from accounts already identified as resellers. Buyers can spend more of their time reviewing listings for the models they are looking for.

## Why customers would pay for Macket

For shops that buy used devices regularly, finding listings is a daily task. Macket offers two practical benefits:

- **Less time spent sourcing.** Buyers can review a filtered list instead of checking each group and reading duplicate posts.
- **Earlier access to suitable listings.** Finding a match sooner gives a shop more time to contact the seller, ask about the device's condition, and decide whether to buy.

Willingness to pay depends on how much time Macket saves and how many useful purchase opportunities it surfaces. Both can be measured when the product is tested with shops.
